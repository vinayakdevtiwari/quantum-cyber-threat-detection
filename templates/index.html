QDS LIVE — BIT MANIPULATION DEMO
Two devices, one quantum backend. Pick a role.

Victim — send & verify

Type a message, sign it, and share the session code with the attacker. You will see what arrives and which bits were corrupted.

Attacker — manipulate bits

Enter the victim's session code, choose an attack, and inject it into the channel mid-transit.

How it works

Your laptop runs the quantum simulation (Qiskit). The phones are remote controls that send instructions and render results — quantum processing cannot run on a phone.

Both devices must be on the same WiFi, and you open the laptop's LAN address (for example http://192.168.1.42:5000) rather than localhost.
